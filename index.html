A computer science student moving deeper into cybersecurity.

I am currently studying for a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science at Université Bourgogne Europe. My academic background gives me a foundation in programming, systems, networks and databases, while my personal work increasingly focuses on cybersecurity.

I am particularly drawn to offensive security: understanding how systems fail, finding weaknesses and learning how they can be secured. I complement my coursework with hands-on labs, CTF-style challenges and structured preparation for the eJPT certification.
